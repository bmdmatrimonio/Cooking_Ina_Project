RECIPE: Homemade Spaghetti

INGREDIENTS:
- 1 pack of 800g pasta (party size)
- 3 tbsp salt
- 1 kg ground pork
- 1 onion
- 1 garlic
- 12 pcs hotdog
- Ground pepper
- 215 g cheese
- 1 900 g pack spaghetti sauce
- 1 250 g tomato sauce
- 5 liters water
- 100 g butter
- Cooking oil

[Prep 1 - Pasta]
TIME: 3 mins + 3 mins buffer

- Get a deep cooking pot that can fit 5 liters of water with some room to spare.
- Pour water into the pot and add salt.

[Process 1 - Pasta]
TIME: 15 mins + 3 mins buffer

- Boil the salted water in the cooking pot.
- Put the pasta in the pot and cook for 8-10 mins or until al dente.

[Finalization 1 - Pasta]
TIME: 2-3 mins + 3 mins buffer

- Put the pasta in a strainer to remove the water.
- Add butter to the cooked pasta and mix thoroughly.

[Prep 2 - Sauce]
TIME: 5 mins + 3 mins buffer

- Clean the ground pork by straining and rinsing it, then set aside.
- Dice the onion and garlic, then set aside.
- Cut the hotdogs into small slices, then set aside.
- Grate 215 g of cheese and set aside.

[Process 2 - Sauce]
TIME: 30 mins + 3 mins buffer

- Heat the pan.
- Add oil, garlic, and onion, then saute.
- Wait until the garlic is approaching golden brown.
- Add the pork and saute.
- Add spaghetti sauce, tomato sauce, and ground pepper.
- Switch to medium heat.
- Wait for the sauce to boil, stirring at intervals.
- Add the hotdog and grated cheese.
- Switch to low heat.
- Stir for 5 mins.

[Finalization 2 - Sauce + Pasta]
TIME: 1-2 mins + 3 mins buffer

- Put the pasta and sauce in a bowl.
- Mix thoroughly.
